# Product Brief — Career360 (Job Portal)

## Product and Audience
- **Product name:** Career360 (Sub-brand: Job Portal)
- **What it does:** End-to-end recruitment and job discovery platform connecting job seekers with verified employers across India's top metropolitan and emerging industrial hubs.
- **Primary audience:**
  1. **Job Seekers (Candidates):** Freshers and experienced professionals looking for transparent salary ranges, verified employer postings, and direct 1-tap applications.
  2. **Employers (Recruiters & HR):** Companies posting vacancies, tracking applications, and screening qualified talent.
  3. **Admins:** Platform operators ensuring employer legitimacy and scam-free listings.
- **Context of use:** Android phones on mobile networks, with tablet layouts supported where practical. The current build target is Android.

## User Jobs
- **Primary job to be done:** Find verified jobs matching skills and location, and apply immediately without deceptive fees.
- **Secondary jobs:** Save jobs for later, track submitted applications, and keep candidate profile details up to date.
- **Most important action on the main screen:** Search/filter jobs and tap "Apply Now".
- **How users know they succeeded:** Immediate visual confirmation ("Application Submitted Successfully" bottom sheet & updated applied status badge).

## Product Truth and Constraints
- **Required features and flows:**
  - Fast light splash screen with subtle app identity branding.
  - Role selection (Job Seeker, Employer ATS, Admin Control).
  - Candidate safety guidelines screen prior to entering candidate mode (preventing job scams, ensuring zero payment required).
  - Feed with Category filters (IT & Software, Marketing, Finance, Design, Healthcare, Education).
  - Search by keywords + Location dropdown.
  - Job card with verified badge, salary range (₹ LPA), location, and 1-tap apply modal.
- **Brand Character:** Professional, Credible, Trustworthy, Modern, Responsive. Full English (no Tamil built-in per client instructions).
- **Design Tokens:** Corporate Blue theme (`#155EEF` primary, `#0B1F3A` dark, `#38BDF8` accent, `#F8FAFC` background, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#172B4D` headings).
