1. Project at a Glance
A job search app for Android, similar to Nithra Jobs. Candidates find and apply for jobs for free. Employers pay a subscription to post jobs. The owner controls everything from an admin dashboard.

3. Job Seeker Features (Free)
3.1 Account
Sign up and log in with mobile number and OTP, or with email and password.
Forgot password and account recovery.
Accept Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
Request account deletion.
3.2 Profile and resume
Personal details: name, mobile, email, location, photo.
Career details: education, experience, skills, current and expected salary, preferred role and location.
Upload, replace or delete resume (PDF, DOC, DOCX).
Profile completion indicator.
3.3 Finding jobs
Home screen: latest, recommended, featured and recently viewed jobs.
Search by job title, keyword, company, skill or location.
Filters: location, category, experience, salary, job type, education, date posted.
Job detail page with description, requirements, salary, benefits, vacancies and deadline.
Save jobs, share job links, report suspicious listings.
3.4 Applying and tracking
Apply using saved profile and resume.
Answer employer screening questions (if enabled).
Duplicate applications to the same job are blocked.

4. Employer / HR Features (Paid)
4.1 Onboarding
Register with HR contact details, email and mobile verification.
Create company profile: name, logo, industry, website, address, description.
Admin verifies the company. Status: Pending → Approved / Rejected / Suspended.
Only approved employers can publish jobs.
Multiple HR users per company (Owner, HR Manager, Recruiter) – optional.
4.2 Dashboard
Active plan name, start and expiry date.
Job quota: total, used and remaining.
Quick actions: Post a Job, Manage Jobs, View Applicants, Company Profile, Subscription, Billing, Support.
Job performance: views, applications, shortlisted candidates.
Warnings when quota is almost finished or the plan is about to expire.
4.3 Posting and managing jobs
Job details: title, category, description, responsibilities, skills, qualification, experience, salary, location, job type, vacancies, deadline.
Work mode: on-site, remote or hybrid.
Save as Draft (does not use quota).
Publish only when the employer is approved, the plan is active and quota remains.
Edit, pause, close, renew or delete a job.

View applicants for each job; filter by date, status, experience, qualification, location.

5.2 Quota rules in simple words
Rule
How it works
Tracking
Quota is tracked per employer. Example: 10 allowed, 4 used, 6 remaining.
What uses quota
One job uses one unit when it is first published. Drafts do not use quota.
Editing
Editing a published job does not use more quota.
Quota finished
Publishing is blocked; employer is asked to renew or buy a plan.
Plan expired
New jobs cannot be published; live jobs are paused or closed (recommended).
Renewal
A successful payment creates or extends the subscription and assigns quota.
Manual changes
Admin can adjust quota only with a reason; every change is logged.
5.3 Recommended default policy
Quota belongs to the purchased period.
Unused posts do not carry forward.
Closing or deleting a job does not give the quota back.
Admin can correct quota manually with a recorded reason.
5.4 Subscription status
Pending Payment, Active, Expired, Cancelled, Suspended, Failed. Payment status is stored separately, so a failed payment can never activate a plan.

9. Payments, Security and Privacy
Payments
One client-approved gateway (for example Razorpay).
Payment is always verified server-to-server; the app's success screen alone is never trusted.
Duplicate payment callbacks cannot give quota twice.
Invoice, receipt and renewal are supported.
GST, invoice format and refund policy must be confirmed by the client.
Security
Role-based access for candidates, employers and admins.
Secure password storage, OTP limits and login throttling.
HTTPS everywhere; all inputs validated.
Resumes and documents are private and shown only to authorized users.
Protection against common attacks (SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, unauthorized API access).
Audit logs for sensitive actions; passwords, OTPs and payment secrets are never logged.
Card details and CVV are never stored.
Privacy
Terms and Privacy Policy acceptance.
Consent controls, data retention rules and account deletion.
10. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The Android app earns from ads through Google AdMob. Google AdSense works only on websites, so AdMob is the correct product for the app, and AdSense can optionally be used on the web panels. Both use the same Google account and pay the client directly.
Ad format
Where it appears

After a job application is submitted, or after several job views

10.1 Ad rules
Ads show to job seekers only. Employer and admin screens stay ad-free (to be confirmed).
No ads on login, OTP, payment, resume upload or application form screens.
Every ad is clearly marked "Ad".
Google test ads are used during development; live ads only after release.
Users are never asked or encouraged to click ads.
10.2 Admin controls
Switch ads on or off for the whole app or for each placement.
Set ad unit IDs, how often native and full-screen ads appear, and which screens show them.
View ad performance (impressions, clicks, estimated earnings) from the AdMob account.
10.3 Setup and compliance
Client creates the Google AdMob account, links the payment profile and approves the app in AdMob.
Privacy Policy is updated to explain ads and the advertising ID.
The Google Play "Data safety" form declares that the app contains ads.
A consent message is shown where the law requires it.
Ads follow Google AdMob and Google Play policies; the app must not click its own ads.
